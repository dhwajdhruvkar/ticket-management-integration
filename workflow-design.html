Three real scenarios at Netlink

How the same engine serves very different parts of the business.

💻

Internal IT helpdesk

Netlink employees (US + India)
  1. Engineer emails "VPN won't connect from home".
  2. Tenant = netlink-internal; category = Network; priority = medium.
  3. RAG hits the Cisco AnyConnect troubleshooting KB article.
  4. Confidence 0.84 → auto-resolved: reply with 4-step fix + citation.
  5. If still broken, requester clicks "Not satisfied" → escalates to IT Ops with full context.
Outcome: deflects ~70% of repeat L1 IT tickets.
👥

Internal HR helpdesk

Netlink employees · HR team
  1. New joiner asks "How many days of casual leave do I get?" via Teams bot.
  2. Tenant = netlink-hr; category = HR / Leave; PII redacted.
  3. RAG retrieves the Netlink leave policy (region-aware: US vs India).
  4. Confidence 0.91 → auto-resolved: reply cites policy section.
  5. Audit block records the exact policy version that answered — proof for HR disputes.
Outcome: HR answers 24×7, consistent policy interpretation.
🏢

Client managed-services desk

External MSP clients · Tier-1 / Tier-2
  1. Client raises "Production job XYZ failed at 03:14" in their portal.
  2. Tenant = acme-corp; tier = Gold (15-min SLA); priority = high.
  3. RAG searches only Acme's KB; finds runbook RB-204.
  4. Confidence 0.72 → drafted; assigned round-robin to Acme's pod in Bhopal.
  5. Engineer approves in 4 min, reply sent, ticket auto-pushed back to client's Jira SM.
Outcome: Gold SLAs met, engineers spend time on real work.
