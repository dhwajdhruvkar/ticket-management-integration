How Helpdesk Works

From ticket to answer in seconds — automatically.

Helpdesk takes incoming tickets, searches your knowledge base, writes a grounded answer, and decides whether to send it, draft it for an agent, or escalate to a human. Every step is logged in a tamper-proof audit trail.

The 5-step pipeline

Each ticket flows through these stages in order, every time.

1

Ticket arrives

A request comes in from email, the portal, or a connected helpdesk (Zendesk, Freshdesk) via webhook.

2

Search the KB

The assistant uses semantic search to find the most relevant knowledge base articles — by meaning, not just keywords.

3

Write an answer

An LLM (Groq · Llama 3.3 70B) writes a step-by-step reply using only the retrieved articles, with citations.

4

Score confidence

Blends how relevant the top match is with how far it beats the runner-up. Score is 0–100%.

5

Decide & act

Auto-resolve, draft for an agent, or escalate to a human — based on the confidence score.

Three possible outcomes

What happens at step 5 depends on the confidence score and a few safety guardrails.

✓ Auto-resolved

Send the reply, close the ticket

The assistant is confident. The answer is delivered to the requester and the ticket is closed — no agent needed.

confidence ≥ 78%
✎ Drafted for agent

Save as a draft, wait for review

The assistant has an answer but isn't sure enough. An agent reviews it, edits if needed, and sends.

confidence 55–78%
↗ Escalated

Send straight to a human

No good match in the knowledge base. The ticket goes to a person to handle from scratch.

confidence < 55%

Safety guardrail: tickets marked urgent are never auto-closed — they're always at least drafted for a human, even when confidence is high.

A real example

This is what actually happens when someone emails “I forgot my password”.

Step 1 — Ticket
Subject: I forgot my password — sent by alex@acme.com via email.
Step 2 — Search
Best match: Reset your corporate password (score 0.81). Runner-up: MFA device replacement (score 0.42).
Step 3 — Answer
“Go to passwordreset.acme.local, click Forgot password, complete the MFA challenge… [1]” — written by the LLM, cites the article.
Step 4 — Score
Relevance 0.81, dominance 0.39 → confidence 87%.
Step 5 — Decision
✓ Auto-resolved — reply sent, ticket closed in 2.4 seconds.

Everything is logged

Every action — receive, search, decide, send — gets a tamper-proof record.

⛓

Tamper-proof audit trail

Each event is hashed and chained to the previous one (like a blockchain). If anyone edits a past record, the chain breaks and the system flags it on the Audit page — perfect for compliance and disputes.
